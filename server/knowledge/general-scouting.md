Walk the field in a W pattern and check at least 10 plants in different places. Note the share of plants affected, whether it is in patches or everywhere, and which leaves (old/new). Photo tips: daylight, leaf filling the frame, one photo of the top surface, one of the underside, one of the whole plant.
