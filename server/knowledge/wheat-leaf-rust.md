**Look for:** small round orange-brown pustules scattered (not in straight lines) mostly on the upper leaf surface; powder rubs off orange-brown. Favoured by warmer (15-25 C), humid conditions.
**Action:** Scout; if pustules reach the upper leaves before grain filling, consult extension office about fungicide and variety advice.
