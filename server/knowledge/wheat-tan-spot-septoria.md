**Look for:** oval to irregular tan/brown lesions often with a yellow halo, which merge into dead patches. No powdery spore mass. Worse after rain/dew, in fields with infected stubble.
**Action:** Cannot be reliably told apart from rusts by description alone - a photo of both leaf sides and an extension worker's check is recommended before any spray.
