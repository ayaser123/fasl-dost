**Look for:** clusters of small soft green/black insects on leaves and ear heads, sticky honeydew, yellowing or curling. Check 10 plants at random in several places.
**Action:** Many fields have natural enemies (ladybirds); spraying only when counts are high per local threshold. Ask extension office for the threshold and product.
