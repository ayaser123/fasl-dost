**Look for:** narrow yellow-orange stripes running along the leaf veins; yellow powder that rubs off on fingers or clothes. Often in patches in the field. Favoured by cool (about 10-15 C), humid or foggy weather with dew.
**Not this if:** spots are scattered round/oval blotches (see tan spot / leaf rust), or whole leaves are uniformly pale (see nitrogen deficiency).
**Action:** Scout the field; note how many plants/leaves show stripes. If confirmed on the flag leaf or spreading, contact the agriculture extension office quickly - timely fungicide (triazole group) on the label-recommended dose is the usual advice. Do not guess product or dose.
**Prevention:** grow rust-tolerant approved varieties; avoid excess nitrogen; report early outbreaks to extension office.
