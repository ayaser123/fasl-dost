**Look for:** older (lower) leaves turn uniformly pale yellow starting at the tip, no spots, no powder; whole field or whole strips look pale, often after heavy irrigation/rain, light soil, or missed fertiliser dose. Waterlogged patches also yellow.
**Action:** Check the field's fertiliser history and drainage. Apply nitrogen only as per local recommendation for the crop stage; ask extension office for the dose. Do not apply extra fertiliser if the problem is rust.
