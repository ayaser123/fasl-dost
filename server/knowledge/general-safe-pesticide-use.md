Never give a spray dose from memory; follow the product label and extension office advice. Do not mix products. Wear gloves, mask; keep children and animals away; do not spray in wind or before rain. Buy only registered products from licensed dealers. If the cause is uncertain, refer to the agriculture extension worker rather than spraying.
